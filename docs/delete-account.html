Delete your Smart Mind account

Smart Mind – a note app that reminds you at the right place and time.

In the app

  1. Open Smart Mind and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

The deletion happens right away and you're signed out.

Without the app

Email REPLACE_WITH_CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my Smart Mind account", from the email address you sign in with. We'll confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • All your notes, including reminders, times, folders and your feedback on them.
  • All your saved places.
  • The copies of your notes our server uses for search.
  • Your sign-in account (email and user ID) in Google Firebase.
  • When you delete in the app: everything the app stored on that phone, including your places' locations.

Nothing is kept. Copies in our hosting providers' backups, if any, are removed when those backups expire. Deleting can't be undone.
